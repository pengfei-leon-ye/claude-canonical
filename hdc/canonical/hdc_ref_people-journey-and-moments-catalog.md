# People Journey and Moments Catalog

- **Status:** transformed chat-project replica; not SOT
- **Active source(s):** `2_topics/hr-digital/journey-and-moments.md`
- **Conversation boundary:** provides a neutral lifecycle vocabulary for HDC discussion; it is not a company Process map, system inventory, initiative backlog, or approved service design

## How to use the catalog

Use the catalog to select lifecycle stages, identify candidate moments, compare coverage, and create persona or industry overlays.

Add a moment to the shared catalog only when it is lifecycle-stable, reusable, clear enough to improve shared language, and not better represented as a persona, local, workforce, industry, or initiative overlay.

## 1. Attract and Awareness

> I notice the organization, become curious, and start to imagine myself working there.

| Moment | Generic description |
|---|---|
| First encounter | I first hear about the organization and form an initial impression. |
| Exploring the organization | I review public information, employee stories, role content, culture signals, and work context to judge fit. |
| Understanding the proposition | I look for clear, honest information on roles, careers, rewards, flexibility, and development. |
| Deciding whether to engage | I decide whether to apply, follow, seek a referral, join a talent pool, or walk away. |

## 2. Apply and Select

> I apply, feel respected and informed, and believe the process is fair and human.

| Moment | Generic description |
|---|---|
| Application | I submit my application and see whether the experience is easy, clear, and acknowledged. |
| Initial response | I learn whether I will move forward; silence is itself an experience. |
| Interview invitation and scheduling | Scheduling feels efficient, considerate, and clear. |
| Interview | I meet interviewers, feel respected, understand the questions, and form a view of the organization. |
| Decision and offer or rejection | The outcome is communicated with appropriate speed, transparency, tone, and respect. |
| Offer details and negotiation | Employment terms, location, relocation, rewards, or other relevant conditions are discussed clearly. |

## 3. Join

> I feel excited, informed, and looked after between acceptance and day one.

| Moment | Generic description |
|---|---|
| Acceptance and welcome | I accept and receive a clear, human welcome. |
| Pre-start communication | I receive role expectations, start details, contacts, and key policy information. |
| Digital pre-boarding | I complete necessary pre-start information easily and securely. |
| Manager and team contact | My future manager or team gives me a practical sense of what to expect. |
| Logistics confirmation | I know where to go, what to prepare, what access I need, and who will help. |
| Returning-hire re-entry | My prior history is recognized without assuming that nothing has changed. |

## 4. Onboard and Integrate

> I feel welcomed, equipped, safe, and clear about my role and how to succeed.

| Moment | Generic description |
|---|---|
| First day | Access, equipment, workspace, schedule, and welcome support are ready. |
| Team and culture welcome | I meet colleagues and understand purpose, values, expectations, and ways of working. |
| Role clarity | My manager explains responsibilities, success measures, and important unwritten norms. |
| Required learning and readiness | I complete required learning and understand what readiness means in my role. |
| Buddy or mentor connection | I have a named person for everyday questions. |
| Early feedback and check-ins | My manager checks progress, gives feedback, and invites feedback on onboarding. |
| Early role decision | Confirmation, extension, redirection, or exit is handled clearly and respectfully. |

## 5. Work, Perform, and Belong

> I can do my job, feel heard and appreciated, and belong to something meaningful.

| Moment | Generic description |
|---|---|
| First meaningful contribution | I complete meaningful work and receive appropriate acknowledgment. |
| Regular manager check-ins | I have predictable time to discuss progress, obstacles, well-being, and development. |
| Goals and performance conversations | Goals and performance are discussed as coaching and alignment, not only administration. |
| Recognition and appreciation | Contribution is noticed and reinforced. |
| Reward moments | Pay, bonus, incentives, or other rewards are explained well enough to understand. |
| Day-to-day support | An HR, payroll, access, workflow, or service issue receives timely, useful support. |
| Life event and well-being support | Personal events or well-being needs are met with clarity, care, and dignity. |
| Manager or team change | Communication protects trust and continuity when reporting lines or teams change. |
| Workplace concern or incident | A concern is handled fairly, safely, and with a learning orientation. |
| Benefits choice or change | I can understand and confidently act on benefits choices or life-event changes. |
| Return from leave or absence | Re-entry, clarity, support, and a realistic ramp-back are designed deliberately. |

## 6. Develop and Grow

> I see a future here and feel the organization invests in my growth.

| Moment | Generic description |
|---|---|
| Discovering learning options | I find relevant development opportunities and understand how to use them. |
| Development planning | My manager and I create a concrete plan covering skills, experiences, and next steps. |
| Structured program participation | I join a development, leadership, graduate, or professional program that is well run. |
| Learning or certification milestone | An important completion is recognized appropriately. |
| Stretch work | I receive challenging work and enough support to succeed. |
| Career conversation | I can explore possible internal paths beyond my current job. |
| Non-selection | Rejection for development or promotion includes constructive feedback or next steps. |

## 7. Move and Evolve

> I can grow through new roles and transitions that feel supported and fair.

| Moment | Generic description |
|---|---|
| Discovering internal opportunities | I can see internal openings, projects, or mobility paths clearly. |
| Internal application | I understand the Process, timing, transparency, and manager involvement. |
| Manager conversations | Current and future managers discuss the move openly and help assess fit. |
| Selection or non-selection | I receive a clear outcome and useful feedback, especially if I remain in my current role. |
| Promotion or role announcement | The new role is communicated and recognized appropriately. |
| Transition and mini-onboarding | I enter the new role with a transition plan suited to the move. |
| Knowledge transfer | I leave the old role with continuity and respect for prior contribution. |
| Early period in the new role | I receive support, feedback, and success signals as responsibilities change. |

## 8. Exit and Alumni

> I leave feeling respected, with my contribution recognized and the future relationship left open.

| Moment | Generic description |
|---|---|
| Decision to leave | The first conversation shapes my final impression. |
| Stay or retention conversation | Possible changes are explored without pressure or guilt. |
| Resignation and notice | Administration is clear and the experience remains respectful. |
| Knowledge transfer | Continuity is planned and my knowledge is valued. |
| Exit feedback | I can give honest feedback and understand how it may be used. |
| Final day | Colleagues and leaders recognize contribution. |
| Access, pay, and benefits closure | Final access, pay, and benefits transitions are handled cleanly. |
| Alumni and possible return | I can stay connected and understand whether future paths remain open. |

## Extension rule

Use an extension instead of changing this catalog when the proposed addition is primarily:

- persona-specific;
- workforce-specific;
- country-specific;
- industry-specific;
- partnership-specific;
- tied to one initiative, product, or temporary operating model.

An extension should reference its parent stage and moment rather than duplicate the whole catalog.

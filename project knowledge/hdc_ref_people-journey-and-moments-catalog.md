# [REF] People Journey and Moments Catalog

- **Project**: HR Digital Cockpit
- **Document Type**: Reference Catalog
- **Status**: Active canonical
- **Role**: Canonical reference catalog of People Journey stages, employee-view stage descriptions, and moments that matter
- **Source Category**: Cat 2
- **Management-System Role**: Reference digest; outside L1-L5 hierarchy; this source is not itself an L2, L3, L4, or L5 artifact
- **Relationship to [OS]**: Reusable reference digest under [OS] §2.1.E; provides a stable stage-and-moment library for People Experience work without owning governing judgment.
- **Relationship to [PRIN] People Experience Design Principles**: The principles source owns reusable design logic. This source owns the stable stage and moment reference spine.
- **Relationship to [PRIN] HR Digital Decision Design Principles**: The digital-decision principles remain the parent cross-topic judgment layer. This source should be used only after People Experience is established as the relevant topic lens.
- **Pairings I participate in**: None currently (per [OS] §8.5.2 as of this revision)

## How to use this source

Use this source when:
- selecting or comparing journey stages
- identifying candidate moments that matter
- checking whether a roadmap or design topic has sufficient lifecycle coverage
- creating persona overlays or prioritization views
- using a stable stage and moment library across People Experience work

Do not use this source as:
- a current-state process map
- a company-specific operating model
- a system inventory
- a release backlog
- a design specification for one initiative
- a substitute for prioritization logic or design principles

## Scope note

This source defines the stable core catalog.

A new stage or moment should be added only when it is:
- lifecycle-stable rather than initiative-specific
- reusable across multiple People Experience topics
- clear enough to improve shared language and design quality
- not better handled as a persona overlay, local variance, or extension note

# 1. Attract & Awareness

> I notice you, become curious, and start to imagine myself working here.

| Moments that matter | Moment description |
| --- | --- |
| First encounter with the brand | When I first hear about you (ad, article, conference, referral) and form an initial, emotional impression. |
| Exploring who you are | When I browse your website, social media, employer brand content, and employee stories to see if your mission, science, culture and locations fit me. |
| Understanding “what’s in it for me” | When I look for clear, honest information on roles, career paths, benefits, flexibility, and development opportunities. |
| Deciding whether to engage | When I ask myself “Is this worth my time?” and choose to apply, follow, join a talent pool, or walk away. |

# 2. Apply & Select

> I apply, feel respected and informed, and believe the process is fair and human.

| Moments that matter | Moment description |
| --- | --- |
| Application experience | When I submit my application and see how easy (or painful) it is, and whether I get immediate acknowledgment. |
| Initial screening & first response | When I hear back quickly—or not—about whether I move forward; silence here is a major negative moment. |
| Interview invitation & scheduling | When scheduling feels efficient, considerate of my time zones and current job, and communication is clear. |
| Interview experience (virtual or onsite) | When I meet interviewers, feel respected, understand the questions, and see how well you represent your culture and scientific / operational reality. |
| Decision & offer / rejection | When you communicate “yes” or “no”: tone, speed, transparency, and how you treat me if I’m not selected all strongly influence my long-term perception. |
| Offer details & negotiation | When we discuss salary, benefits, title, location, visa/relocation (if relevant); I judge how flexible and transparent you are. |

# 3. Join (Offer Acceptance to Day One)

> I feel excited, informed and looked after between offer and first day.

| Moments that matter | Moment description |
| --- | --- |
| Offer acceptance & celebration | When I sign and feel welcomed (e.g. a congratulations message from the hiring manager or leadership). |
| Welcome & pre-start communication | When I receive a clear welcome pack (role expectations, start date, site/location info, contacts, key policies). |
| Digital pre-boarding | When I can easily complete paperwork (contracts, background checks, personal data, bank details, tax, right-to-work, etc.) online. |
| Pre-start engagement with manager & team | When my future manager reaches out, maybe introduces the team, shares a rough plan for my first week. |
| Logistics confirmation | When I get practical details: where to go, what to bring / wear, parking / shuttle info, security / access arrangements. |

# 4. Onboard & Integrate (First 3-6 Months)

> I feel welcomed, equipped, safe, and clear about my role and how to succeed.

| Moments that matter | Moment description |
| --- | --- |
| First day experience | When I arrive and see whether my badge, IT, workspace, PPE / lab coat, etc. are ready—and whether someone is genuinely happy to see me. |
| Welcome to team & culture | When I’m introduced to colleagues, hear the “story” of the company, mission, values, and how my work connects to patients and customers. |
| Role clarity & expectations | When my manager explains my responsibilities, metrics for success, and the “unwritten rules” of how we work here. |
| Mandatory training | When I complete required compliance / safety / GMP / SOP training and understand how to work safely and correctly in my environment. |
| Buddy / mentor connection | When I have a named person I can go to with “small” questions—this often makes or breaks how supported I feel. |
| Early feedback & check-ins (e.g., 30/60/90 days) | When my manager explicitly checks how I’m doing, gives feedback, and invites my feedback on the onboarding itself. |
| Probation / confirmation decision | When I learn if I’m confirmed in role, and how that conversation is handled—clear, developmental, and respectful or not. |

# 5. Work, Perform & Belong (Day-to-Day Experience)

> I feel able to do my job, heard, appreciated, and part of something.

| Moments that matter | Moment description |
| --- | --- |
| First “real” contribution / early win | When I successfully complete an experiment, batch, project or client deliverable and get acknowledgement. |
| Regular 1:1s with my manager | When I have predictable time to talk about work progress, obstacles, wellbeing, and development—not only once a year. |
| Performance & goal-setting conversations | When we set and review goals (annual or quarterly), and I see whether this is a box-ticking exercise or a real coaching dialogue. |
| Recognition & appreciation | When I receive thanks, public recognition, awards or simply a genuine “well done,” and when others see great work celebrated. |
| Reward moments (pay review, bonus) | When I see if pay and bonuses feel fair relative to my contribution and the market, and whether rationale is transparent. |
| Day-to-day support & problem resolution | When I hit an issue (HR query, payroll error, system glitch, lab / plant issue) and see how quickly and helpfully it’s resolved. |
| Life events & wellbeing support | When I experience things like parental leave, illness, burnout, bereavement, or personal crises and feel (or don’t feel) that the company genuinely supports me. |
| Manager or team changes / reorganizations | When my manager changes or my team is restructured, and communication and transition support shape my sense of security and trust. |
| Ethics, safety or quality incident | When something goes wrong (near miss, deviation, complaint) and I see whether the culture is blame-oriented or learning-oriented. |
| Benefits enrollment / life-event benefits change | When I need to choose, change, or understand benefits because of annual enrollment or a life event, and the experience helps me make clear, confident decisions. |
| Return from leave / post-parental leave / return to work | When I come back after an extended leave and need clarity, support, and a structured re-entry into work, not just administrative reinstatement. |

# 6. Develop & Grow (Skills & Career)

> I see a future here and feel the company invests in my growth.

| Moments that matter | Moment description |
| --- | --- |
| Access to learning & development options | When I first discover the learning ecosystem (LMS, academies, on-the-job learning, conferences) and see if it’s relevant and accessible. |
| Creation of my development plan | When my manager and I co-create a concrete plan (skills, experiences, roles) rather than generic “grow as a leader” statements. |
| Enrollment in structured programs | When I join a technical, leadership, or graduate program and see if it’s well-run and aligned to real career paths. |
| Completion of key training / certification | When I pass a qualification (e.g. GMP, safety, technical, project management) and it’s recognized as a milestone. |
| Stretch assignments & cross-functional projects | When I’m trusted to lead or contribute to something challenging (new product, tech transfer, client project) and get support to succeed. |
| Career conversations (e.g., annually or at milestones) | When someone asks, “Where do you want to go next?” and helps me explore internal paths, not just my current job. |
| Not being selected for development / promotion opportunity | When I’m turned down for a program or role, and how feedback and next steps are handled (demotivating or growth-focused). |

# 7. Move & Evolve (Internal Mobility, Promotion, Role Change)

> I can grow through new roles and transitions that feel supported and fair.

| Moments that matter | Moment description |
| --- | --- |
| Becoming aware of internal opportunities | When I can see internal openings, gigs, and projects clearly (via internal job boards, talent marketplaces, manager sharing). |
| Internal application & transparency | When I apply for an internal move and understand the process, timelines, and how my current manager is involved. |
| Conversations with current & future managers | When both managers talk openly with me about the move, support it (or don’t), and help me assess fit. |
| Selection / non-selection for internal role | When I get a “yes” or “no” and how feedback is given (especially important if I stay in my current role). |
| Promotion or new role announcement | When my new role is communicated—to me and to others—and whether it’s celebrated as a milestone. |
| Transition & mini-onboarding into the new role | When I start, meet new colleagues, learn new processes, and get a structured ramp-up plan tailored to the move (not just “you already know the company”). |
| Handover & knowledge transfer from old role | When I leave my previous position and transfer critical knowledge, ensuring continuity and showing respect for what I built. |
| First 90 days in new role | When I’m evaluated and supported in my new responsibilities; early wins here strongly influence my confidence and retention. |

# 8. Exit & Alumni

> I leave feeling respected, with my contributions recognized, and the door open.

| Moments that matter | Moment description |
| --- | --- |
| Decision to leave & first conversation with manager | When I share that I’m considering leaving or have an external offer, and my manager’s reaction shapes my final impression. |
| Stay / retention conversation (if appropriate) | When the company explores whether conditions can change to keep me—without pressure or guilt. |
| Formal resignation & notice process | When I hand in notice and see how administratively easy and emotionally respectful the process is. |
| Handover & knowledge transfer | When I’m asked to document and pass on my work in a structured way, and my knowledge is clearly valued. |
| Exit interview / feedback moment | When I’m invited to share honest feedback, and I sense whether it will genuinely be used to improve things. |
| Final day experience | When colleagues and leaders recognize my contribution (goodbye messages, small gatherings, thank-you notes) rather than me quietly disappearing. |
| Offboarding from systems, pay & benefits | When access, equipment return, final pay, benefits continuation / conversion are handled cleanly and transparently. |
| Alumni & potential boomerang relationship | When I’m invited to stay connected (alumni network, newsletters, events) and it’s clear I’d be welcomed back if paths cross again. |

## Extension guidance

Use an extension instead of changing the core catalog when the proposed addition is mainly:
- persona-specific
- workforce-scope specific, such as contingent labor
- country-specific
- industry-specific
- collaboration-context specific rather than lifecycle-stable

Examples that may belong in extensions depending on context:
- field or lab-worker-only moments
- works-council-sensitive change moments
- expatriation or assignment-prep sub-moments under internal mobility
- return-to-site, hybrid-work, or office-based coordination moments
